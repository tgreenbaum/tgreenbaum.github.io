Content and the customer journey

Buyers don't need the same thing at every step. Select a stage to see what buyers are thinking, the content that helps them, the intent to target, and the metrics that show it's working.

Select a stage, or use the arrow keys

Awareness: top of funnel

Buyers are experiencing a problem or pain and seek info and resources to better understand it, give it a name, and alleviate it—without yet knowing of a solution.

Content that educates, informs, engages, shows thought leadership, introduces our brand, and addresses common struggles, pain points, and questions

Common types

  • Blogs
  • Infographics
  • Social posts
  • Short videos
  • Webinars (ungated)
  • Ebooks (ungated)

Themes

  • High-level overviews
  • Basic strategies
  • Quick tips
  • Broad recs
  • Industry stats & trends
  • How-tos

Examples of intent

  • “How to print stamps and labels”
  • “Discount shipping rates for ecomm”
  • “How to manage returns”
  • “High volume label printing for warehouses”
  • “Ship faster during busy seasons”

Metrics

  • SEO
  • Traffic
  • Impressions
  • Engagement
  • Views

Consideration: middle of funnel

Buyers have defined and named their problem and are starting to explore potential solutions.

Content that builds trust, addresses specific challenges and audience segments, and provides the information and proof-points they need to evaluate their options

Common types

  • Blogs
  • Infographics
  • Guides, Checklists, Tools, and Comparison charts
  • Case studies
  • Social posts
  • Emails
  • Webinars (gated)
  • Ebooks (gated)
  • Whitepapers & Reports (gated)
  • Product demo videos

Themes

  • In-depth strategies and tips, and specific recs
  • Deep-dive of industry stats and trends
  • Product differentiators
  • First-party data
  • Key findings from research

Examples of intent

  • “Top shipping software compatible w/ Shopify”
  • “Direct carrier vs. multi carrier”
  • “Real time inventory and order tracking”
  • “Tools to optimize shipping workflow”
  • “Integrating returns with ecomm platform”

Metrics

  • Downloads and form-fills
  • Conversion rate
  • Email open rates
  • Engagement
  • Lead qualification and nurturing

Decision: bottom of funnel

Buyers have decided on their solution strategy, method, or approach, and their goal is to compile a list of available vendors.

Content that is personalized, targets decision-makers, handles objections, offers reassurance, instills buyer confidence, and compels action or purchase

Common types

  • Testimonials and reviews
  • Sales decks
  • Case studies

Themes

  • Free trials
  • Live demos
  • Social proof

Examples of intent

  • “ShipStation vs. ShipHero”
  • “Metapack demo”
  • “Shipping software pricing plans”

Metrics

  • Pipeline, Opps, MQLs
  • CPA
  • Revenue and other sales KPIs
